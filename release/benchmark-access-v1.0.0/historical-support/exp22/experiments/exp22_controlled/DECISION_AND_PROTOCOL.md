**Duplicate decision:** A complete equal-time comparison of the proposed method set does **not** exist. Repeating EXP11's 56 audit-valid tasks would duplicate work, so this pilot selects exactly its 22 INVALID tasks by sealed audit identity. Their previous order bytes and objectives remain uncredited. The EXP22 controller reserves 0.5 seconds (or half of a one-second cap) for termination; the original worker, method parameters, seeds, input bytes, and deadlines remain frozen. This is **repair of an incomplete pilot**, not independent graph replication. The resulting small Xiong–IPSNS comparison can establish feasibility and a conditional five-graph time-to-quality view; it cannot supply the proposed broader contemporary comparison. All EXP11 files stay immutable. No queued or running equivalent job was found before this decision.

## D. Frozen pilot and conditional production protocol

**Scientific question:** On identical directed weighted graphs, what valid complete-order objective is available by a common elapsed-wall checkpoint, and how often does each method finish or provide a valid incumbent? The missing evidence is a validated end-to-end common-time Xiong–IPSNS feasibility comparison; a broader comparison would additionally need matched LR-TA, WMSF, Eades, and initialized insertion arms. The manuscript currently makes no universal winner or equal-time claim.

**Frozen pilot graph set:** EXP11's four synthetic views `exp04_er_n120_k05_r01` (`87fa70b1…`), `exp04_cm_n120_k05_r01` (`e9b5aa45…`), `exp04_sf_n120_k05_r01` (`2c699a90…`), `exp04_er_n480_k05_r01` (`637dd769…`), and one CollegeMsg full-count view (`e5cc9809…`). Full graph hashes and byte hashes are in EXP11's checksum-pinned private manifest. Synthetic IDs are reused EXP04 topology and weight bytes; CollegeMsg is one EXP05 source parent. There are five graph views, **not five independent real-world parents**. No graph is generated or rewritten. All methods in a paired row use the exact same weighted graph. Ali is outside the pilot because its parent structure is unresolved.

**Pilot signature:** EXP11 task budget/seeds/method configuration exactly; only 22 audit-invalid tasks rerun. Xiong single and repeated original RASstar versus frozen IPSNS with 200 iterations and WMSF/L2 seed initialization. Synthetic caps 1, 10, 30 s; CollegeMsg 120 s; seeds 1 and 2. Distinct restart seeds follow EXP11's saved rule. At each cap, credit only an independently validated complete ordering checkpointed before the cap. Xiong restarts may retain a completed incumbent even if a later restart is killed. IPSNS exposes a valid output only on final return, so no trajectory is imputed from progress text. Repeated full fresh runs at 1/10/30 s are **not** milestones of one run. Aggregate seed observations within each graph/cap, then compare graph-level paired signs and normalized objective differences `(B_A-B_B)/T` only where both arms have valid outcomes. Report status and time-to-first-valid order. No significance test or population claim on five selected views. Early-finished order may be carried forward at later checkpoints only if it is a saved valid incumbent from the **same** run; no cross-run carry forward.

**Runtime and correctness:** Monotonic task wall begins before worker launch; conversion, loading, algorithm initialization, solver/refinement, validation and controller overhead count. Child CPU user/system and process wall are separate. A timeout has no objective unless a complete, before-deadline event passed independent audit; failure/invalid statuses persist. The frozen auditor reopens graph bytes, confirms normalization and full vertex permutation, recomputes weighted backward arcs and retained DAG, checks saved objective and order hash, and rejects cap overruns. Self-loops are absent in selected strict DIMACS graphs; future empirical inputs must report loop constants separately. One Wulver general/low CPU, one thread/affinity, 8 GB and 30-minute Slurm allocation; hardware/model, Python, node, job ID and environment enter manifest. This requests less than 11 minutes of summed task caps plus startup/audit margin. The pilot produces operational feasibility evidence only until its final independent audit succeeds.

**Recovery:** Output path must be new and absent. Frozen source and original EXP11 output are read only. Selection is deterministic from SHA-256-checked original manifest/inventory/audit, requiring exactly 22 invalid tasks with total 636 s of caps. Checkpoints are per task. A failed or interrupted recovery leaves completed outputs untouched; a future job may select only still-invalid/missing tasks after a new documented audit. Never overwrite and never reinterpret the 22 original INVALID rows. A combined analysis must link each task to either its original audit-valid checkpoint or its separately audited recovery checkpoint and retain both job IDs. The existing EXP11 audit code is the independent post-run verifier; its nonzero exit blocks scientific completion.

### Bounded pilot correction after first submission

The first submitted recovery job, `1426749`, reached RUNNING on `n0006` but **FAILED** after 47 seconds. It checkpointed its first 10-second Xiong-restarts task within cap (9.504 s); a separate invocation of the frozen independent auditor verified that one task, 1,388 complete restart events, its objective and graph/order records, with no violation. The second 30-second task took 30.641 seconds under a 0.5-second termination reserve, so it remains INVALID. The original EXP11 task and the first failed recovery output are immutable. This job was **not** observed healthy for two minutes.

The evidence demonstrates that 0.5 seconds is too little shutdown margin on the shared filesystem. The corrected controller reserves three seconds and selects only the **15 still-unattempted or invalid tasks with 10/30/120-second caps**, excluding the independently audited first recovery task. The six invalid one-second tasks remain INVALID because scheduler and filesystem overhead make them an unreliable end-to-end science checkpoint; no objective is imputed. The corrected selection sums to 620 seconds of task caps. It requires the exact prior hashes and the first-task audit/checkpoint hashes before creating a new output directory. This amendment was recorded before the second submission and supersedes the 22-task launch plan above; the scientific question and original method source/seed/graph contracts remain unchanged. A second cap overrun or fatal audit will end EXP22 pilot submission attempts rather than trigger a resubmission loop.

**Conditional production recommendation:** No production launch in EXP22. If this repair validates end-to-end caps and useful checkpoint coverage, freeze a *new* stratified 24-graph candidate before outcomes: three synthetic families × two sizes × two weight regimes (12 topology/weight views nested in six topology cells), plus at most 12 graph views from at least four known empirical parents, selected by identity and structure rather than observed winner. Use LR-TA, WMSF/L2, weighted Eades, original Xiong, IPSNS, and both insertion initializers, all with full initialization cost and one CPU per task. Two stochastic seeds, caps 10/30/120 s chosen after pilot feasibility. This is an **estimate** of at most 24×7×2×(10+30+120) = 53,760 CPU-s (14.9 CPU-h) before load/audit overhead; deterministic arms need only one seed, so actual allocation should be smaller after manifest finalization. Parent-level descriptive intervals require enough genuinely distinct parents; 12 views over four parents cannot justify a broad population test. EXP07 graph reuse and paired weights remain dependent. Reassess whether this evidence changes a manuscript claim before spending those resources. Public release of synthetic inputs/orders remains gated by the separate NJIT rights determination; internal analysis may continue, and no external communication is authorized.
