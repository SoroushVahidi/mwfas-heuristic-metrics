# EXP25 resource estimate, access boundary and approval gates

## Conservative task and allocation matrix

The core has 19 substantive views × (five deterministic one-seed arms + three stochastic two-seed arms) × three fresh caps, plus six small paired-weight diagnostic views × the same 11 tasks at 10 seconds only = **693 tasks**. The five deterministic arms are LR-TA, WMSF/L2, weighted Eades, and two insertion arms. The three two-seed arms are IPSNS, Xiong single, and Xiong restarts. All arms use one CPU at a time. The cap sum is `19 × 11 × (10+30+120) + 6 × 11 × 10 = 34,100` seconds = **9.47 nominal CPU-hours**, not an observed runtime or an equal-work claim. These caps include method-side launch/loading/conversion/initialization; cleanup and post-run audit need additional allocation time.

The table is a **ceiling model**, not expected consumption: a deterministic arm that finishes early releases its unused cap. At 10 seconds, a 20-second post-task allowance is intentionally conservative and outside the scientific deadline; it covers filesystem/controller shutdown between tasks. The independent auditor is also outside the method deadline. The full optional plan reserves at most **35 one-CPU allocation-hours**, while modeled core-plus-optional consumption is 25.50 hours. The 21 audited EXP13 120-second cells on three selected views are potential imports, never automatic new outcomes; if all contracts match, new core work falls from 693 to 672 tasks and nominal caps from 34,100 to 31,580 seconds, with only one credit per unique graph/method/seed/cap identity. The exact bundle balance and Slurm limits must be checked against current Wulver policy immediately before authorization. Five-view bundles must be balanced by estimated caps; a bundle of five full-cap views models 15,100 seconds (4.19 h), leaving 49 minutes in a five-hour request. Use one core/thread, CPU affinity, **8 GB RAM**, `general`/`low` as in audited pilot precedent, and record actual model, memory and queue. Do not fan out many simultaneous jobs or assume allocation availability. No job is submitted in EXP25.

Historical anchors: EXP11/22's 10/30/120-second controller exposed invalid one-second shutdown behavior; the corrected 15-task recovery took 10:13 on one EPYC 7763 CPU. EXP13's seven methods on three empirical parents completed a 21-task Slurm job in 7:56 with 8 GB requested; two insertion tasks timed out. On selected EXP24 views, Xiong produces early full orders while IPSNS's first available order is conditional and absent on ER480/CollegeMsg at tested caps. These observations justify budgeting to full caps and validating RSS/timeout behavior, **not** extrapolating a reliable speed ranking across different hardware or larger graphs. The 7,115-vertex full Wiki-Vote graph is deliberately excluded; the selected largest-SCC view remains 1,300 vertices. The IRS view has 2,864 vertices and 54,522 arcs and is the principal memory/time risk. If the compatibility pilot exceeds 8 GB or lacks meaningful paired availability, reduce/stratify the design before a full run rather than silently changing cap or method settings.
