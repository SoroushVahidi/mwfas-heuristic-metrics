# EXP25 controlled-time benchmark: private preflight, 18 October 2026

**Decision: MODIFY DESIGN; do not launch.** A broader matched-time comparison is advantageous for the JDSA paper, not necessary for a defensible submission. The manuscript already reports a qualified, independently audited source-parent-aware benchmark. A new study could establish whether the EXP24 quality/availability tradeoff persists across more graph regimes and seven identified empirical parents, and whether the project LR-TA, WMSF/L2, weighted Eades and insertion arms change that comparison. No such finding exists yet. The immediate submission gate is stronger objective-witness access and accurate literature positioning, not more task records. The author should review this design and its rights/access implications before any separate execution task.

## Previous evidence and duplicate decision

EXP03–04 provide generated family comparisons under iteration budgets; EXP05–07 provide four empirical parents, dense economic matrices, paired weights and 198+45 numerical references, with EXP07 reusing 45 existing graphs. EXP08 measures IPSNS seed/startup behavior. EXP09 tested one Rocket-Crane C component and EXP09B blocked fidelity for the full pipeline. EXP10 compared authenticated Xiong RASstar against IPSNS with unequal algorithmic work; EXP12/13 compared Xiong, LR-TA, WMSF/L2, insertion and Eades at a 120-second cap on 20 Ali hashes and three new empirical parents, without IPSNS. Ali's number of independent source parents remains unresolved. EXP14–21 establish graph identities, rights classes, public metadata, three Wikimedia graph bytes, ten verified orders, and a verifier; they are not a controlled-time contemporary method campaign.

EXP11/22/24 already constitute the exact five-view controlled pilot. The frozen audit has **78 unique task identities, 72 audit-valid statuses, 61 usable objectives, and six INVALID one-second tasks**. Among the 72, 37 are PASS and 35 valid TIMEOUT; a timeout need not lack an objective. IPSNS has lower backward weight on each of the three 120-vertex synthetic views at 10 and 30 seconds, with dependent seed and cap repeats. ER480 and CollegeMsg lack paired IPSNS objectives; no quality winner exists there. The tested authors' `RASstar` is their FASDC variant with `r=1/3`, applied to weighted inputs; it is not every algorithm in Xiong et al. No pooled significance or population inference follows. See [EXP24](../exp22_controlled/EXP24_INDEPENDENT_ANALYSIS.md). The six invalid cases remain preserved and are not proposed for rerun.

The 25 candidate graph hashes in `candidate_graphs.csv` are existing, individually validated graph bytes. The 12 EXP04 selections use `r02` and degree 3/8, avoiding the EXP11 `r01/k05` views; the six EXP06 selections are three same-topology pairs of uniform versus capped-lognormal weights; the seven real views are one per identified source parent. They are *new method-by-time combinations*, not new graph observations or a claim that EXP07 reuse supplies independence. EXP13 already supplies **21 independently audited 120-second cells** (19 PASS, two insertion TIMEOUT) on three of the selected real graph hashes: six Xiong single seed cells and 15 deterministic cells. Execution planning must reuse each order/clock record **only if** controller definition, environment/hardware class, parameter and checkpoint contracts match. Otherwise a new run must be explicitly labeled a protocol change, not duplicate credit. The core 693-task resource ceiling includes these 21 as potential reruns; a compatible reuse would require only 672 new core tasks and 2,520 fewer nominal cap seconds. The pilot should first test only novel method/graph/cap cells.

## Frozen candidate input rule

`candidate_graphs.csv` freezes 25 exact IDs, canonical weighted hashes, byte SHA-256, topology identity where the inherited contract provides one, source parent, `n`, `m`, density, SCC count/largest SCC, total weight, weight spread and redistribution class. A read-only preflight recomputed these structural statistics from the frozen private DIMACS bytes, checked all 25 byte hashes and `n,m,total_weight`, and found 25 unique weighted hashes and seven empirical parents. No graph/order byte or absolute Wulver path is included here. EXP13's inherited metadata does not provide the cross-campaign topology hash; its graph byte/hash and independent source parent are frozen under its distinct normalization contract, and `source_derived` is **not** a claimed topology hash.

Selection was made by family, `n` (28/120/480 for synthetic views), degree 3/8 for EXP04, paired weight regime on EXP06, and one view from each source parent. It did not use new method outcomes. The three EXP06 pairs are deliberately small weight-sensitivity diagnostics and count as **three topology units**, not six independent graphs. The four EXP05 sources and three EXP13 sources give seven source-parent units; application-domain coverage is still narrow, and the seven parents are not a probability sample. CollegeMsg's selected 90-day view is nested in its full source, and each empirical parent contributes only one view. `candidate_graphs.csv` is a private provenance table; its rights classes are not grants.

## Frozen method and time protocol for a later authorization

Core eight labeled arms: project LR-TA, project WMSF/L2, project weighted Eades/igraph adapter, project IPSNS initialized by WMSF/L2, authors' Xiong `RASstar` single invocation, the same executable under a separately labeled intentional restart schedule, project LR-TA plus insertion, and project WMSF/L2 plus insertion. Five deterministic arms have one task; IPSNS and each Xiong configuration have seeds 1 and 2. The Xiong restart sequence must retain the EXP11 recurrence and save only fully completed before-deadline orders. IPSNS keeps its frozen `L2`, 200-iteration, top-15-SCC settings; initial construction counts within the cap. Insertion starts from its named full-order constructor, has at most 1,000 accepted moves, and must replace EXP12's fixed 105-second internal deadline by the remaining task deadline for every cap. This is an implementation gate, not permission to alter historical runs. No additional algorithm joins the core merely because it appears in a paper.

Run independent fresh tasks at **10, 30 and 120 seconds** on the 12 EXP04 and seven empirical views. The six tiny EXP06 paired-weight views receive **10 seconds only** as a structural/weight diagnostic; repeatedly spending 30/120 seconds on these 28-vertex views would mainly add intentional restart runtime without useful new independent units. One-second tasks are removed. For a given graph/cap every arm receives the same elapsed-wall ceiling, including process launch, graph loading, conversion, initialization, refinement, serialization and controller shutdown. A safe reserve must be tested before production; a cap overrun is INVALID, never relabeled TIMEOUT. CPU time, wall time, core affinity, node CPU model, memory, environment lock, source/binary hashes, seed, exit status and Slurm allocation are separate fields. An early-finished valid order may be held to its own later checkpoint only within one continuous run; the proposed fresh 10/30/120 tasks are separate experiments and are never stitched into a trajectory. Single-shot, restart-until-cap, and final-only arms remain visibly different.

The larger real-source stratum may add a **prespecified separate 300-second cap for all eight arms on all seven real views**, but only if a separately authorized compatibility pilot obtains one complete, independently valid IPSNS order on the prespecified dense LODES view by 300 seconds without memory/deadline fault. This is an **availability gate**, independent of which method's objective is better. If it fails, omit the long stratum and report limited real-parent quality pairing. Report both the core and any supplemental stratum, without pooling different caps as replicates.

The independent auditor must reopen each input at its frozen byte hash, check canonical identity and normalization, all declared vertices exactly once, weighted backward arcs by independent integer arithmetic, retained DAG acyclicity, self-loop constant as recorded, saved objective equality, complete-order checkpoint hash, monotonic event deadline and strict end-to-end wall cap. Missing/incomplete order means no objective. Keep PASS, valid TIMEOUT with objective, valid TIMEOUT without objective, FAILED, INVALID and MISSING distinct. Numerical gaps are shown only for matching valid numerical certificates; EXP07 bounds are not automatically exact. Report objective availability before quality. Normalize paired differences by the same graph's total active arc weight; show graph-level paired signs and values, then collapse repeated seeds and weight views within topology and real views within parent. Descriptive ranges and parent-wise displays are appropriate; no significance test is prespecified for seven selected real parents.

## Minimal nonduplicating compatibility pilot, not authorized here

`exp04_cm_n480_k08_r02` and `exp13_lodes_de_commute` test generated and dense source-derived conversion plus major memory behavior. Run the eight arms on the synthetic view at a 30-second cap (11 tasks), and on LODES run one previously untested IPSNS seed 1 at 300 seconds plus bounded parser/checker smoke checks for the existing arms. This adds no redundant EXP13 deterministic outcome. Its **630 nominal cap seconds**, 12 tasks, one CPU, 8 GB and at most one hour of requested wall time test checkpoint capture, deadline reserve, initialization, serial resource accounting, independent audit and the optional-cap availability gate. A second IPSNS seed belongs to the later study, not this smallest operational pilot. If the pilot passes final audit and its code/hardware/parameter/deadline contract is identical to production, import each relevant task by unique identity rather than rerun or count it twice. Freeze the exact pilot manifest and compare active/queued signatures before any future submission. Pilot outcomes do not enter the manuscript or the 5,254-outcome extension automatically.

## Scientific and editorial decision

**Manuscript now:** no new numerical claim. An EXP25 result could justify a compact availability-plus-paired-quality table and a time-to-first-valid-order figure if it produces audited matched observations across several real parents; if pairing remains absent, it supports only a transparent reliability/feasibility statement. It would not support a universal best method or a new heuristic contribution. **Additional benchmark:** MODIFY DESIGN before approval: test the deadline/controller adaptations and cost on the novel-cell pilot, decide the optional long cap based on feasibility, and settle reviewer-access scope. The author may instead submit the current EXP23 manuscript after strengthening its literature review and artifact-access documentation. No EXP25 task or Slurm job was launched.
