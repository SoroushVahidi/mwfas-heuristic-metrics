# Figure captions

`figures/structural_coverage.pdf`: Directed-density and cyclic-SCC arc coverage against represented vertex count for all 442 records. Colors distinguish author-generated inputs, traceable empirical views and the mixed-origin Ali collection. Points may overlap, particularly paired weights on the same topology; records are not independent samples. Both axes in panel (a), and the vertex axis in panel (b), are logarithmic.

`figures/source_dependence.pdf`: Raw shares of the 44 traceable empirical views by eight named parent lineages, compared with the equal-parent share of 12.5%. The line is a descriptive weighting choice, not a sampling probability or independence guarantee. Ali is excluded from this empirical-parent denominator.

`figures/weight_and_reference_coverage.pdf`: Degree/weight concentration (a) and reference availability by topology-balanced size bins (b). Panel (a) uses the same source classes as the structural figure. Panel (b) counts records; 99 EXP06 topology pairs therefore each contribute two weight records. Certificates are numerical, not exact-arithmetic proofs. The largest bin has two certified graphs, shown by a callout.

Each figure is also supplied as SVG and 300-dpi PNG. No manuscript figure was replaced.
